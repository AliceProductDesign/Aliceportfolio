Methods

I build clean, human-centered experiences that move people, working at the intersection of visual excellence and rigorous product thinking. My process pairs qualitative research and usability testing with systems-level design, turning ambiguous problems into scalable design systems and interfaces that hold up in production. I lead cross-functionally from early discovery through developer handoff, treating stakeholder alignment and clear documentation as part of the craft, not an afterthought.
